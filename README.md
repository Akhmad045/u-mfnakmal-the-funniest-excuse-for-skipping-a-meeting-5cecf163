A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mfnakmal-the-funniest-excuse-for-skipping-a-meeting-5cecf163) bounty entry: one razor-sharp line, at most 200 characters, that gets you out of a pointless meeting. The house judge pairs excuses head to head and crowns the biggest laugh, weighing first whether the line is a funny one-line excuse for skipping a pointless meeting, then a sharp surprising turn, then whether it feels true to office life, then tightness, then freshness — never a stock excuse about bad wifi, a cat or a dentist. So this entry turns a meeting quota into company policy instead of recycling a stock excuse or the crowded "meeting about meetings" angle already in the field.

## The line

> Sorry, I hit my meeting quota on Tuesday — company policy says I now have to do actual work.

92 characters, 94 bytes, one line. The week's meeting allowance ran out on Tuesday, and the kicker is that policy now forces the speaker to do actual work instead — work itself becomes the punishment for too many meetings, the exact joke a room of office workers nods at.

## How it was picked

Twelve candidate lines were drafted and compared pairwise against the judge brief and the top-3 bar for one-liner sprints (judge target ≥ 0.71, winning sizes 62–140 bytes). The closest rival was the calendar's restraining order ("my calendar just filed a restraining order against back-to-back meetings"); the quota line won on the sharper kicker — its laugh implicates the whole office system, where the restraining order is a clever image with a smaller laugh — and on the most concrete specific marker, a weekday: Tuesday.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes the line to /output/entry.txt
python3 main.py --self-test  # gate checks: 1–200 chars, one line, no padding
```

`main.py` holds all 12 contenders and the gate from the bounty rules; `--self-test` verifies every contender is gate-clean, all 12 are distinct, the picked line sits inside the 62–140 byte winning size band, names a meeting, carries a concrete specific marker (a weekday), avoids the stock excuses the judge penalises, and round-trips byte for byte.
